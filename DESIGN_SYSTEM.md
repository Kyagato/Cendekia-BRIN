## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, accessible, and structured visual language for national public sector knowledge management. Developed to serve government apparatus, researchers, institutional partners, and the public, the design system projects competence, transparency, institutional credibility, and effortless searchability.

### Design Movement: Institutional Modernism
The design system operates at the intersection of modern corporate minimalism and public service utility:
- **Clarity over ornament:** Structured white cards, precise hairline boundaries, and rigorous vertical alignment ensure high information density remains clean and scannable.
- **Deep sovereign blue gradients:** Hero spaces and key brand areas use deep royal and navy blues (`#1e40af` through `#2563eb`), anchoring the portal with a trusted governmental posture while introducing modern digital vibrance.
- **Systematic categorization:** Color-coded micro-indicators, pill chips, and structured taxonomies empower public servants to rapidly identify media formats (Teks, Video, Audio, Gambar) and administrative categories (Tata Kelola, Layanan, Aplikasi).

## Colors

The color architecture relies on high-contrast digital blues paired with clean architectural neutrals.

### Surface and Structure
- **Canvas Base:** `#f8fafc` provides a balanced, low-glare backdrop distinct from pure white `#ffffff`, allowing content cards and search modules to lift subtly off the page.
- **Card Surfaces:** Solid pure white `#ffffff` with hairline `#e2e8f0` framing creates modular containment for knowledge items.

### Primary Spectrum
- **Primary (`#2563eb`):** Applied to primary actions, interactive elements, focused input borders, and text media identifiers.
- **Secondary (`#1e40af`):** Used for solid headers, active hover states, and structural iconography.
- **Deep Gradients:** Hero modules deploy a linear transition from `hero-blue-deep` (`#1e3a8a`) down to `hero-blue-vivid` (`#2563eb`), enriched with delicate ambient radial spotlights for depth.

### Media & Classification Chips
Functional semantic colors provide immediate recognition of content types:
- **Teks:** Azure tint (`#eff6ff`) with `#2563eb` text and icon.
- **Video:** Soft crimson tint (`#fef2f2`) with `#dc2626` text and icon.
- **Audio:** Crisp emerald tint (`#f0fdf4`) with `#16a34a` text and icon.
- **Gambar:** Warm amber tint (`#fefce8`) with `#ca8a04` text and icon.
- **Institutional Audience (e.g., Layanan Pemdi):** Calm neutral slate (`#f1f5f9`) with `#475569` text.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. It offers pristine legibility on low-resolution government displays and mobile screens while retaining an authoritative, institutional weight in heavier cuts.

### Typography Hierarchy & Rules
- **Hero Title:** High-impact bolding (`headline-hero`) with negative tracking (`-0.02em`) conveys national presence.
- **Section Titles:** Paired with a standard subtitle (`body-sm` in `#475569`) clarifying the contextual filter or domain.
- **Card Headings (`headline-sm`):** Limited to two lines with strict ellipsis truncation to preserve uniform grid card heights.
- **Micro Labels:** Pill badges and card dates use `label-sm` and `caption` respectively, maintaining crisp optical balance against bordered containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a maximum content container width of `1280px`.

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) page margins.
  - Featured items: 2-column or 3-column layouts.
  - Standard knowledge cards: 4 columns (3 cards per row).
  - Category index cards: 5 columns per row or 4 columns per row depending on tier.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.5rem` margins. Cards reflow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, `1rem` margins. Cards reflow to a single full-width stack.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Section spacing follows an expanded `space-xl` interval (`2.5rem` to `3.5rem`) to maintain clear differentiation between Knowledge Highlights (*Pengetahuan Unggulan*), Knowledge Recents (*Pengetahuan Terbaru*), and Category Directories (*Kategori Populer*).
- Card internal padding is fixed at `space-md` (`1rem`) to ensure maximum information density without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system favors a flat, low-contrast boundary aesthetic with selective elevation states. This ensures performance across public network infrastructures and preserves high accessibility.

### Hierarchy of Surfaces
1. **Flat Plane (Default Cards):** Cards sit at `0px` elevation, delineated solely by a 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`) against the `#f8fafc` background.
2. **Elevated Plane (Hero Search & Dropdowns):** Floating search bars within the hero and global navigation dropdowns utilize soft ambient diffusion:
   - `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
3. **Interactive Hover Lift:** Knowledge and category cards transition subtly upon hover:
   - Border shifts from `#e2e8f0` to `#cbd5e1`.
   - Box-shadow shifts to `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
   - Subtle vertical translation of `-2px`.
4. **Hero Canvas Wave:** The royal blue hero container uses an organic gentle SVG wave base separator to transition naturally into the light `#f8fafc` content canvas.

## Shapes

The design system balances sharp institutional precision with user-friendly accessibility via roundedness level `2`.

- **Cards & Containers:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius ensures modern cohesion while preserving structural card boundaries.
- **Pill Badges & Tabs:** Full round / pill shaping (`9999px`) applied to media type badges, category counters, and category filter chips.
- **Search Modules:** Inner-pill search elements (`9999px`) in the hero section provide welcoming focus, whereas header mini-search fields employ `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Buttons:** Main interactive CTA buttons use `0.5rem` (8px) for official operations, with primary search submit using pill styling within the search container.

## Components

### Hero Search Module
- **Container:** Integrated pill search box nested within the deep gradient hero. Solid `#ffffff` background with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` or `#e2e8f0`.
- **Search Input:** Clean, unbordered inline field with magnifying glass icon in `#94a3b8` and placeholder `Cari pengetahuan...`.
- **Primary Submit:** Pill button (`#2563eb`, hover `#1d4ed8`) positioned inline at the right edge of the input.

### Knowledge Item Card
- **Surface:** Solid `#ffffff`, `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`, `padding: 1rem`.
- **Header Badges:** Two pill badges aligned horizontally:
  - Media badge on left (e.g., `Teks`, `Video`) with matching semantic background and icon.
  - Target audience or department on right (e.g., `Layanan Pemdi`) in neutral slate badge (`#f1f5f9`).
- **Title:** `headline-sm`, line-height 1.4, colored in `#0f172a`, hoverable with `#2563eb` color transition.
- **Metadata Row:** Calendar icon + date stamp (e.g., `10 September 2026`) in `caption` font and `#94a3b8`.
- **Tags Footer:** Subordinate metadata tags prefixed with small outline tag icons in `#64748b`, wrap-aligned at the base.

### Category Directory Card
- **Surface:** Solid `#ffffff`, `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`, `padding: 1.25rem`.
- **Icon Container:** Rounded square (`44px × 44px`) with `#eff6ff` background and vibrant `#2563eb` outline stroke iconography (open book/shield/database).
- **Title & Description:** Bold `headline-sm` title followed by 2-line truncated summary in `body-sm` (`#475569`).
- **Count Badge:** Solid `#f8fafc` bottom pill or bordered chip displaying knowledge tally (e.g., `151 Pengetahuan`) in bold `label-sm`.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2563eb`, white text, `height: 40px`, `px-4`, font-weight 600. Hover: `#1d4ed8`. Active: `#1e40af`.
- **Outline / Secondary:** White background, `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text `#1e293b`. Hover: `#f8fafc`, border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#2563eb` or `#475569` with background transparent. Hover: `#eff6ff`.

### Filter Chips & Media Badges
- **Filter Tabs:** Segmented row displaying categories (`Semua`, `Teks`, `Gambar`, `Video`, `Audio`).
  - Active: Solid `#1e293b` or `#2563eb` text with border-bottom or full pill background.
  - Inactive: Clean `#ffffff` with border `#e2e8f0` and `#64748b` text.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Input Fields:** `height: 40px`, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`.
- **Focus Ring:** `outline: 2px solid #2563eb; outline-offset: 1px;`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp `0.25rem` radius on checkboxes and full circles on radios. Accent color matches `#2563eb`.
